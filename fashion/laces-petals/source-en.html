My Journey Through Color

This collection continues the journey I began last season—a return to the souks of my childhood, where my mother taught me to touch fabrics,
discover color and trust my instincts. This season, color leads me from
the souk into a garden that feels joyful, fresh and alive.
The palette is inspired by fresh fruits and garden flowers: the soft green
of pistachio, the juicy pink of watermelon and the sweetness of fraise.
These bright, happy tones carry their own meaning and expression—a
feeling of optimism, abundance and good fortune.
The collection gives you the sensation of sipping a cup of Lebanese white
coffee—a delicate blend of orange blossom water and hot water. Clear,
fragrant and refreshing, it is a familiar ritual and a small moment of pure
pleasure.
That same feeling of freshness and lightness flows through the
silhouettes. The shapes are simple, feminine and free. In the special
couture dresses, pink, mint and blue bloom through luminous beads,
delicate fringe, airy tulle and cascading layers that move softly around
the body.
Rooted in my memories and culture but expressed with a modern spirit,
this collection is my journey through color—a celebration of freshness,
movement and joy.

Extraordinary Moments Around the World

Resort 2027 is inspired by a woman whose life is shaped by travel, culture, and meaningful experiences. Drawing from some of the world’s most beautiful destinations, the collection blends the elegance of Venetian palazzos with the rich craftsmanship and traditions of the Mediterranean.

Architectural embroideries, intricate handwork, and luxurious textures are balanced with clean silhouettes and modern proportions. A palette of jewel tones, coral, sea-glass aqua, emerald, blush, and silver feels both timeless and fresh.

Venice serves as a subtle influence throughout, with ornamental motifs inspired by historic palaces and decorative arts reimagined through a contemporary lens. Feathers, metallic embroidery, hand beading, and statement jewelry details add dimension and individuality.

Resort 2027 celebrates beauty, culture, and exceptional craftsmanship for life’s most memorable occasions. A wardrobe shaped by travel, culture, and extraordinary moments.

Exotic Wardrobe for the Traveling Women

•••• ••••’s Spring 2026 Ready-to-Wear collection tells an emotional story of a woman traveling the world with a single jacquard trunk, discovering her identity through the garments inside. Inspired by global gardens, the collection features blooming florals across luxurious fabrics like Mikado, tweed, brocade, and cotton poplin, with references ranging from English bouquets to serene Japanese landscapes.

The color palette reflects nature’s softness and vibrancy, blending pale blues, pinks, apricots, and mint with bold bursts of turquoise, coral, and golden yellow, grounded by earthy neutrals and shimmering metallics.

Signature embellishments—embroideries, sculptural braids, and layered appliqués of flowers and birds—add depth and storytelling, enhanced by ornate jewelry and floral crowns. The house’s bird emblem evolves into a phoenix-like motif, symbolizing freedom, intuition, perseverance, and ultimately, renewal and hope.
